# Product

## Register

product

## Users
A single operator at a desk. They have accumulated hundreds of agent skills across Cursor, Claude, Codex, and other tool chains. They open the cabinet to see what is installed, read a skill before trusting it, and quarantine what should not be active.

## Product Purpose
Skill Cabinet is a local catalog for installed agent skills. Success is being able to find a skill quickly, understand it (body, frontmatter, files), and move it out of an active drawer into quarantine without ceremony or irreversible loss.

## Brand Personality
Quiet, precise, librarian. The interface should feel like a working catalog, not a product. Confidence comes from order and legibility, not decoration.

## Anti-references
SaaS dashboards. Neon-on-dark "dev tools." Inter. Identical icon-and-caption card grids. Glassmorphism. Cute confirmations for destructive actions.

## Design Principles
1. The skill body is the object: give it a readable measure and keep chrome out of the way.
2. Scopes are drawers; skills are cards; metadata is cataloguing, not a settings panel.
3. Destructive action is a quarantine stamp, not a cute confirmation.
4. Density is allowed in the drawers; emptiness belongs to the reading surface.
5. Every label should sound like a library, not a startup.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast. No additional motion or colour-blindness requirements beyond that.
